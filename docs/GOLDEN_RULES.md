# AEGIS AI Golden Rules

These principles guide every architectural and engineering decision.

---

## Rule 1

If AEGIS cannot explain it,
AEGIS cannot recommend it.

---

## Rule 2

Store facts.
Compute knowledge.

---

## Rule 3

Domain objects should be immutable whenever practical.

---

## Rule 4

Every recommendation must be traceable back to evidence.

---

## Rule 5

Build for understanding before automation.

---

## Rule 6

The domain owns the language.
External systems adapt to it.

---

## Rule 7

Every module should have one clear responsibility.

---

## Rule 8

Make invalid states impossible.

---

## Rule 9

Quality is never sacrificed for speed.

---

## Rule 10

Every important feature deserves automated tests.
