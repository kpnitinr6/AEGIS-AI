# AEGIS AI - Product Requirements Document

## Vision

AEGIS AI is an institutional-grade trading analysis platform designed to help traders understand the market rather than blindly follow trading signals.

The platform combines market structure analysis, Smart Money Concepts (SMC), liquidity analysis, AI reasoning, and professional risk management into one unified system.

---

# Mission

Do not predict the market.

Understand it.

---

# Primary Goals

- Read live market data.
- Detect market structure.
- Detect liquidity.
- Detect Smart Money Concepts.
- Explain every trade.
- Execute trades only when confidence is high.
- Help traders become better decision makers.

---

# Design Principles

- Explain every decision.
- Never hide uncertainty.
- Prefer quality over quantity.
- Every module must be testable.
- AI supports the trader, not replaces the trader.

---

# Version 1.0 Scope

- MT5 Integration
- Market Engine
- Smart Money Engine
- Liquidity Engine
- AI Confidence Engine
- Trade Execution
- Risk Management
- Desktop Dashboard
